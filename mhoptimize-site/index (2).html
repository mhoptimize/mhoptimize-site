About

The operator in your corner.

I've spent more than 25 years growing businesses at every stage, from first hire to global footprint.

My career began in New York's financial industry, including Fortune 500 firms, where I built operational and administrative teams, launched marketing and human resources departments, and managed projects of $5M+, including a $1M office buildout above Rockefeller Center.

As COO of a tech startup, I grew the company from 1 to 25 employees, expanded it from 3 to 20 countries, and took a prototype to a shippable product in under 12 months. So I know what scaling feels like from the inside, not just on a slide.

Outside the boardroom, I've volunteered more than 25,000 hours in humanitarian aid: running short-term free medical clinics and primary education programs, and coordinating with local governments and partners across Africa, Central America, New York City, and beyond. It turns out logistics in the field and logistics in a startup have a lot in common.
